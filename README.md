# Google Developer Group WOW Pune

## About
Welcome to the GitHub repository for Google Developer Group (GDG) Women of Wearables (WOW) Pune! This community is dedicated to supporting and empowering women and diverse individuals in technology, with a special focus on wearable technology, AR/VR, IoT, and other emerging technologies.

## Mission
Our mission is to create an inclusive environment for learning, collaboration, and growth in the tech community of Pune. We strive to:
- Foster diversity and inclusion in technology
- Provide hands-on learning experiences
- Connect members with industry experts
- Showcase innovative projects and ideas
- Support career development in tech

## Events
We organize various events including:
- Technical workshops
- Coding sessions
- Hackathons
- Speaker sessions
- Networking meetups

## Get Involved
