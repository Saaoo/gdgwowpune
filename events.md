## How to Register

For upcoming events, registration opens approximately two weeks before the event date. To register:

1. Visit our [Meetup page](#)
2. Find the event you're interested in
3. Click the "RSVP" button
4. Complete the registration form

## Propose an Event

Have an idea for an event? We welcome community suggestions! Please submit your proposal by:

1. Opening an issue in this repository
2. Using the "Event Proposal" template
3. Filling in all the requested details

Our team will review your proposal and get back to you within a week.

## Code of Conduct

All attendees, speakers, sponsors, and volunteers at our events are required to agree with our [Code of Conduct](https://developers.google.com/community-guidelines). Organizers will enforce this code throughout the event.
